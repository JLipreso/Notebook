# Q-013 — Monetization loop & the meaning of "Free"

The brief prices students (₱69/₱129) and teachers (₱89/₱169) with a 14-day free tier. Two open product questions: (1) which side drives growth, (2) what happens on day 15.

## 13a — Growth loop

**Option A — Teacher-led (recommended).** A paying teacher's students get course access regardless of their own tier; students pay for notebook-side premium (more notebooks, storage, fonts, AI).
- ✅ One teacher converts 30–40 students into users at once; matches how classroom tools actually spread (Google Classroom, Quipper).
- ❌ Teacher price may need to reflect the value of "your whole class included".

**Option B — Both sides pay independently (brief as written).** Students need a paid tier to keep using courses after trial.
- ✅ Two revenue streams from day one.
- ❌ One non-paying student fragments a class — the teacher's experience breaks, and the teacher is the churn risk that matters.

**Option C — Student-led.** Notebook is marketed straight to students; classroom layer is the upsell.
- ✅ Largest audience.
- ❌ ₱69/month from PH students with no teacher pull is the hardest sell in this market.

## 13b — What does "Free" mean on day 15?

**Option A — Free becomes a permanent limited tier (recommended):** e.g. 2 active notebooks, read-only access to archives, join courses but limited features. Data is NEVER deleted or locked away entirely.
- ✅ "Your notes are held hostage" headlines are fatal for a notebook product; permanent free floor keeps the funnel alive.

**Option B — Hard lock after 14 days (brief as written):** account read-only or inactive until payment.
- ❌ Risky for trust; also collides with manual GCash verification delays (a paid user can sit "Unverified" for days).

**Decision (13a):** Option A — teacher-led: a paying teacher's students get course access regardless of their own tier; students pay for notebook-side premium. Locked 2026-09-13 as **D-022** (Lead Developer via option prompt).

**Decision (13b):** Option A — Free becomes a permanent limited tier after day 14 (e.g. 2 active notebooks, read-only archives, limited course features); user data is never deleted or fully locked away. Locked 2026-09-13 as **D-023** (Lead Developer via option prompt).
